# Blank Canvas
A relaxing puzzle walking simulator with a unique painting mehcanism for revealing the world within the painting.

Play the game for free in Steam: [Blank Canvas](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4608340/Blank_Canvas/)

## Synopsis
You are a painter, living in modest apartment. Paintings are your passion. Empty canvas awaits your imagination. Enter the blank world of the canvas and reveal the hidden world around you and find out what the passion wants to reveal.

## Description
Unique game where you need to find a key item inside painting in order to finish the level. Switch completed canvas into new one in order to start new level.

#### Explore the painted world
Use your brush to reveal the world around you. Find the unwritten story left behind.

#### Simple puzzles
Find helpful environmental tips to guide you, jump over the platforms. Pickable items can be used to reach otherwise unreachable places.

#### Multiple worlds
The bedroom is your main world from which you can enter into multiple unique canvas worlds.
